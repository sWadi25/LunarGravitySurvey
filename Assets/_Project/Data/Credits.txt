<b>3D MODELS</b>
"Simple Low Poly Lunar Lander" (https://skfb.ly/oQ9TM) by Trockk,
licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0).
Modifications: rescaled and re-centred.

Terrain, craters, rocks, starfield, sample container and scanner:
generated / built by the team (original work).

<b>TEXTURES</b>
Earth: NASA "Blue Marble" imagery (public domain),
via three-globe example images; clouds added by the team.
Sun, starfield, regolith, dust: generated by the team.

<b>AUDIO</b>
All sound effects synthesised by the team (original work).
Radio intro tone recreates NASA Apollo's 2525 Hz "Quindar" tone.
Houston voice lines: generated with macOS text-to-speech (Apple).

<b>SOFTWARE & TOOLKITS</b>
Unity 6, XR Interaction Toolkit, OpenXR, Input System, VR Template (Unity Technologies);
TextMesh Pro fonts Liberation Sans & Inter (SIL Open Font License).

<b>SCIENCE REFERENCES</b>
Lunar surface gravity 1.62 m/s² - NASA Moon Fact Sheet
Sample classes simplified from Apollo lunar sample data (NASA)
