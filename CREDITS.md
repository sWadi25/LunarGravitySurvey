# Credits

`Assets/_Project/Data/Credits.txt` (the in-game credits board) is a shorter version of this file: it leaves out *AI assistance* (disclosed in presentation segment 10) and condenses *Software and toolkits* so it fits on the board. Keep everything else identical.

## 3D models
- "Simple Low Poly Lunar Lander" (https://skfb.ly/oQ9TM) by Trockk is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Modifications: rescaled and re-centred.
- Terrain, craters, rocks, starfield, sample container and scanner: generated or built by the team (original work).

## Textures
- Earth: NASA Visible Earth "Blue Marble" imagery (public domain, NASA Earth Observatory), via the three-globe example images (github.com/vasturiano/three-globe). Modifications: resized, procedural cloud layer added by the team.
- Sun disc, starfield, regolith, dust and visor textures: generated by the team (original work).

## Audio
- All sound effects (scanner, container, radio static, beacon, dosimeter, footsteps, suit fan) were synthesised by the team with `Tools/generate_sfx.py` (original work, no licence needed). The 2525 Hz radio intro tone recreates NASA Apollo Mission Control's "Quindar" tone.
- Houston voice lines: generated with macOS text-to-speech (`say`, Apple) via `Tools/generate_houston_voice.sh`

## Software and toolkits
- Unity 6 (6000.6), XR Interaction Toolkit, OpenXR Plugin, Input System, VR Template sample assets (Unity Technologies)
- Liberation Sans and Inter fonts (SIL Open Font License)

## Science references
- Lunar surface gravity 1.62 m/s²: NASA Moon Fact Sheet
- Rock classes simplified from Apollo lunar sample data (NASA)
